⚠ Please read first: Full access

Full access includes the ability to delete transactions and records. Ideally nobody has Full access – it should be limited to Administrators only. For everyone else, please choose the lowest level they need to do their job.
View – can look but not change anything
Create – can view and add new records
Edit – can view, create and change existing records
Full – everything above plus delete (administrators only)
